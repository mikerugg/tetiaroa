## Environment — ocean.png

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: panoramic 2D game background for a children's ocean exploration game.
Primary request: Paint one beautifully art-directed underwater world as a very wide 3:1 panoramic illustration, 3072x1024 if possible. Side-on cutaway view entirely below the ocean surface. The left third is a sunlit tropical coral garden in turquoise with pink finger corals, golden sponges and creamy sand. The middle third opens into clear teal-blue water with layered distant sea fans and hanging rock shapes at the very bottom. The right third gradually becomes mysterious indigo open water with a few violet rock silhouettes at the bottom. Keep the upper 65 percent of the entire image mostly open water with soft dappled light, beautiful watercolor washes, tiny flecks, and spacious room for separately animated submarine and animal sprites. Reef detail grows from the bottom edge, never fills the center. Foreground edges lush and tactile.
Style/medium: premium children's natural history picture book, hand-painted gouache and colored pencil edges, subtle paper grain, playful organic shapes with sophisticated painterly detail; entirely 2D, no 3D.
Lighting/mood: wondrous, inviting, luminous, immersive. Strong turquoise/coral contrast on left, beautiful moody lavender/ink blue on right, never gloomy.
Constraints: single continuous seamless composition, no panels or borders. NO animals, NO fish, NO submarines, NO text, NO letters, NO UI, NO watermark. This is the environment layer only. Wide horizontal 3:1 aspect ratio.
