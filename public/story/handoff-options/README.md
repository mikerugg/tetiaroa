# Handoff image directions

These generated images are illustrative concept artwork, not documentary records of specific events. Each old/new pair was designed for the panoramic scroll reveal in the Our Story homepage concept, with the left side kept clear for overlaid copy.

## 01 — The Lagoon Witness

- Old: an anonymous early-1960s visitor, seen from behind, encounters a low Polynesian atoll while carrying a period film camera.
- New: two contemporary Polynesian field researchers survey the same lagoon from the same camera position.
- Direction: quiet continuity; one person’s proposition becomes shared stewardship.

## 02 — The Landing

- Old: an anonymous early-1960s visitor wades ashore from a modest wooden skiff.
- New: contemporary Polynesian conservation workers bring biosecurity cases and survey gear ashore from a small field boat.
- Direction: arrival becomes action.

## 03 — The Field Notes

- Old: anonymous hands hold a weathered notebook beside a period camera at the lagoon’s edge.
- New: a contemporary researcher records observations beside a water-quality probe and sample vials.
- Direction: an idea becomes evidence.

## Shared generation constraints

- Photorealistic documentary treatment with a restrained, natural atmosphere.
- Approximately 2.5:1 panoramic framing at 1983 × 793 pixels.
- Low coral atoll geography with no mountains or resort structures.
- Primary detail confined to the right side; left 60% reserved for website copy.
- No identifiable celebrity likeness, embedded text, logos, trademarks, or watermarks.
- Archival images use period-plausible early-1960s materials and warm monochrome grain.
- Modern images preserve the paired archival camera geometry and use practical, unbranded field equipment.
